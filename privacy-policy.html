Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 9, 2026

1. Introduction

Maconomics ("we," "our," or "us") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect information when you visit https://life.maconomics.com/ or interact with our services.

2. Information We Collect

We may collect the following information when you voluntarily provide it through our website, forms, or other communications:

  • Name and contact information
  • Email address and mobile phone number
  • Information submitted through website forms
  • Communication preferences and consent records
  • Other information you voluntarily provide when contacting us

We may also collect limited technical information, such as browser type, device information, and website usage data.

3. How We Use Your Information

We may use your information to:

  • Respond to inquiries and requests
  • Provide information about our services, programs, and offerings
  • Send service-related notifications and updates
  • Send promotional communications when you have provided the required consent
  • Improve our website, services, and customer experience
  • Maintain communication preferences and comply with applicable legal requirements

4. SMS/Text Messaging Privacy

If you provide your mobile phone number and expressly consent to receive text messages from Maconomics, we may send you messages relating to the program you selected, including informational updates, service-related communications, or promotional messages, depending on your consent.

Message frequency may vary depending on the program and your interactions with us. Message and data rates may apply.

You may opt out of SMS communications at any time by replying STOP to a message. For assistance, reply HELP or contact us through our website.

Providing consent to receive text messages is not a condition of purchasing any goods or services.

Mobile Information and Messaging Consent Protection: No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. All the above categories exclude text messaging originator opt-in data and consent; this information will not be shared with any third parties. We may share information with service providers that help us deliver our communications and operate our services, solely as necessary to perform those functions and subject to appropriate safeguards.

5. Sharing of Information

We do not sell your personal information. We may disclose information to trusted service providers who assist with website hosting, customer support, communications, analytics, and other business operations, as necessary to provide our services.

These service providers are expected to use the information only for authorized business purposes and to protect it appropriately.

Mobile phone numbers and SMS opt-in or consent data are not shared with third parties or affiliates for their own marketing or promotional purposes.

We may also disclose information when required by law or when necessary to protect our rights, users, or services.

6. Cookies and Website Analytics

Our website may use cookies and similar technologies to improve website functionality, understand visitor activity, and enhance the user experience. You can manage cookies through your browser settings.

7. Data Security and Retention

We take reasonable administrative and technical measures to protect personal information from unauthorized access, loss, misuse, or disclosure. We retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this policy or as required by applicable law.

8. Your Privacy Choices

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information, subject to applicable legal requirements.

You may unsubscribe from marketing emails using the unsubscribe link included in those emails. You may opt out of text messages by replying STOP.

9. Third-Party Websites

Our website may contain links to third-party websites. We are not responsible for the privacy practices or content of external websites. We encourage you to review their privacy policies before providing personal information.

10. Children's Privacy

Our services are not intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 through our website.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be published on this page with an updated effective date.

12. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how your information is handled, please contact Maconomics through the contact information provided on our website: https://life.maconomics.com/
